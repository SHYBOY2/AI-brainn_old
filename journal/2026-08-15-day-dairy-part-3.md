📝 Gym Notes
Today's session started differently than I expected. Even though I told my regular gym partner that I would arrive around 5:40 PM, he didn't wait for me. Instead, he trained with a group of Hindi-speaking guys. Since he had already started his workout, I ended up training with a new guy named Sanjay, who is currently studying at Andhra University.
Training with Sanjay made me realize how different our training philosophies are. He follows a 5-day training split with relatively low volume, while I train 6 days per week with significantly higher volume and intensity. Since I'm a lean lifter with slightly visible abs, my biggest limitation right now is recovery and nutrition. If anything, I should probably reduce my training volume slightly to recover better. Sanjay, on the other hand, would likely benefit from increasing both his training volume and intensity.
