### Active Recall
Active recall is the process of actively recalling information from memory. It involves testing oneself, quizzing, summarizing, and self-testing to reinforce learning and improve retention.

#### Benefits
The benefits of active recall include:
* Improves retention
* Strengthens memory
* Enhances understanding

#### Contrast to Passive Re-reading
Active recall is in contrast to the passive re-reading of material, which does not engage the learner as effectively and can lead to superficial understanding."
