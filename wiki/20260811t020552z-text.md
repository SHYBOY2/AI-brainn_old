### Introduction to Dagestan Wrestling
Dagestan Wrestling originates from Dagestan, Russia, and encompasses both Freestyle and Greco-Roman wrestling styles.

### Key Aspects
The key aspects of Dagestan Wrestling include:
* Emphasis on physical conditioning and strength
* Focus on takedowns and throws
* Incorporation of traditional Caucasian wrestling techniques

### Notable Wrestlers
Some notable wrestlers from Dagestan include:
* Khabib Nurmagomedov, who is also a renowned MMA fighter
* Abdulrashid Sadulaev, an Olympic and world champion

### Training Characteristics
The training characteristics of Dagestan Wrestling are marked by:
* High-intensity training sessions
* Focus on live wrestling and sparring
* Incorporation of strength and conditioning exercises
