### Proper Pushup Form Using Shoulder Blade
To perform a pushup with proper form, focus on the following key points:

* **Engage scapula**: squeeze shoulder blades together 
* **Scapular rotation**: rotate downward and outward 
* **Chest position**: keep chest up and forward 
* **Shoulder stability**: maintain stable shoulder joint 
* **Core engagement**: engage core muscles for support 
* **Proper form**: avoid letting shoulders roll forward or sag
