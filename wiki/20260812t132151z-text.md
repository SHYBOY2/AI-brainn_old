# Process
A process is a series of actions or operations taken to achieve a specific end.

## Definition
The definition of a process involves a set of key elements, including:
* **Inputs**: The resources or materials used to initiate the process.
* **Transformations**: The actions or operations performed on the inputs to produce the desired outcome.
* **Outputs**: The resulting products or services generated by the process.

## Types of Processes
There are several types of processes, including:
* **Business processes**: Related to the operations and management of organizations.
* **Industrial processes**: Involved in the production and manufacturing of goods.
* **Natural processes**: Occurring naturally in the environment, such as ecological or geological processes.

## Characteristics
Processes can be characterized as:
* **Sequential**: Occurring in a linear sequence, with each step building on the previous one.
* **Parallel**: Multiple tasks or operations happening simultaneously.
* **Iterative**: Involving repetition of a sequence of steps, with each iteration refining the outcome.

## Goals of a Process
The primary goals of a process are to achieve:
* **Efficiency**: Minimizing waste and optimizing resource utilization.
* **Effectiveness**: Producing the desired outcome and meeting objectives.
* **Quality**: Ensuring the output meets the required standards and specifications.
