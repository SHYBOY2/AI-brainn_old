### Introduction to Research AI Agent
A research AI agent is a software system that utilizes artificial intelligence and machine learning to assist in research tasks. This page provides an overview of how to build and utilize a research AI agent.

### Free/Cost-Effective Options
To get started with building a research AI agent, consider the following free or cost-effective options:
* Utilize open-source AI frameworks such as TensorFlow and PyTorch
* Leverage cloud services with free tiers, including Google Colab and AWS Free Tier
* Explore pre-trained models and fine-tune them for specific tasks

### Building a Research AI Agent
To build a research AI agent, follow these steps:
1. **Define research goals and objectives**: Determine the purpose and scope of the research AI agent
2. **Choose a programming language and framework**: Select a suitable programming language (e.g., Python) and framework for building the AI agent
3. **Develop a data pipeline**: Create a data pipeline for data collection and preprocessing
4. **Train and evaluate AI models**: Train and evaluate AI models using the collected data

* **Utilize natural language processing (NLP)**: Use NLP for text analysis and summarization

3. **Establish a feedback loop**: Establish a feedback loop for model evaluation and improvement
