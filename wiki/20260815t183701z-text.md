## Definition
A process is a series of actions or operations performed to achieve a specific goal or outcome.

## Purpose
The primary purpose of a process is to accomplish a particular objective by transforming inputs into outputs.

## Characteristics
Processes typically exhibit the following characteristics:
* Sequential or parallel steps
* Inputs and outputs
* Transformations or changes

## Types of Processes
There are several types of processes, including:
* Business process
* Industrial process
* Mental process

## Key Elements
The key elements of a process include:
* **Inputs**: The raw materials, data, or information used to initiate the process
* **Processing**: The series of actions or operations performed to transform the inputs
* **Outputs**: The resulting products, services, or information generated by the process
* **Feedback**: The mechanism by which the process is evaluated and improved to ensure it is achieving its intended goal
