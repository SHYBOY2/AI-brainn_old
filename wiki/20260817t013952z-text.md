## Definition
A process is a series of actions or operations taken to achieve a specific end.

## Key Elements
The key elements of a process include:
* **Inputs**: The resources or materials used to initiate the process
* **Transformations**: The actions or operations performed on the inputs to achieve the desired outcome
* **Outputs**: The results or products generated by the process

## Types of Processes
There are several types of processes, including:
* **Business Process**: A process used to achieve a business goal or objective
* **Industrial Process**: A process used in manufacturing or production
* **Natural Process**: A process that occurs naturally, without human intervention

## Characteristics of Processes
Processes can be characterized as:
* **Sequential**: A series of actions performed in a linear sequence
* **Parallel**: Multiple actions performed simultaneously
* **Iterative**: A process that involves repeated cycles of action and feedback

## Goals of Processes
The primary goals of a process are to achieve:
* **Efficiency**: Minimizing waste and optimizing resource utilization
* **Effectiveness**: Achieving the desired outcome or result
* **Quality**: Ensuring the output meets the required standards or specifications
