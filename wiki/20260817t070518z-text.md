## Definition
A process is a series of actions or operations.

## Key Elements
The key elements of a process include:
* inputs
* transformations
* outputs

## Types
There are several types of processes, including:
* business process
* industrial process
* mental process

## Characteristics
Processes can be characterized as:
* sequential or parallel
* repeatable or non-repeatable
* measurable or non-measurable
